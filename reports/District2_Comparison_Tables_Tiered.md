### DISTRICT CONTEXT

District 2 is Labour's most loyal stronghold — the Cottonera heartland of Birgu, Senglea and Bormla, historically the most working-class and harbour-industrial area of Malta. Labour has won 65–70% of the vote here in every post-2013 election, with PN consistently below 30%. In 2022 PN elected only one MP (Stephen Spiteri); Labour typically takes four seats and sometimes sweeps five. The **expected split is 4 Labour / 1 PN**, with any other outcome structurally impossible under current polling.

Robert Abela, Prime Minister and Labour leader, personally contests D2 (and D5), following the precedent set by his predecessor Joseph Muscat. Abela's presence here is more symbolic than electorally necessary — his first-count vote in 2022 was 2,980 fewer than Muscat's had been in 2017, signalling that the Muscat personal vote was considerably stronger in this district. This gap will be watched closely in 2026.

Key local issues include the ongoing Grand Harbour regeneration, cost of living, and the broader immigration/policing context under Home Affairs Minister Byron Camilleri, who also contests here.

*Note: The 2026 district boundaries incorporate part of Marsaskala, giving John Baptist Camilleri (PN) a local councillor's foothold that was less present in 2022.*
