### DISTRICT CONTEXT

District 5 is the southernmost arc of Malta — the rural coastal belt of Żurrieq, Qrendi, Birżebbuġa, Kirkop, Safi and Mqabba. Labour has dominated here since 2013 with 63–66% of the vote; PN typically polls 32–35%. The expected seat split is **3–4 Labour / 1–2 PN**. In 2022 Labour took 3 seats and PN 2, with the district the most competitive of the deep-south belt.

The defining feature of D5 in 2026 is the **battle of party leaders**: Robert Abela (Labour, PM) and the former PN leader Bernard Grech contest this district as their primary/secondary battleground. Abela contests D2 and D5; Grech contests D11 but historically has links to D5. Crucially, **Miriam Dalli** (Energy Minister) is the Labour anchor candidate here, and her controversial is-Siċċa Birżebbuġa project (pledged in 2022, still unbuilt in 2026) will be locally scrutinised.

On the PN side, **Toni Bezzina** and **Stanley Zammit** are the incumbents; **Conrad Borg Manché** (former Labour mayor, defected to PN) adds a dramatic crossover element. Conrad Borg Manché's primary district is D10 — his D5 listing is secondary.

**Key local issue:** Overdevelopment and coastal access (Birżebbuġa maritime area), cost of living, and the unrealised is-Siċċa regeneration pledge.
