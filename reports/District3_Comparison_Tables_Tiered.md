### DISTRICT CONTEXT

District 3 covers Labour's second most loyal southern stronghold — the rural and semi-urban belt of Żejtun, Għaxaq, Marsaxlokk, and part of Marsaskala. Labour has consistently won 65–70% of the vote here since 2013. The expected split is **4 Labour / 1 PN**, the same as in 2022.

The 2026 race is structurally reshaped by two major absences: **Carmelo Mifsud Bonnici (PN)** has retired after 28 years in parliament, leaving no natural PN candidate to absorb his votes; and the district loses the dominant Fearne personal vote from the cabinet era (Fearne now contests as a backbencher without ministerial profile, while awaiting trial). On Labour's side, the most contested dynamic is whether **Chris Fearne's** return — fighting criminal fraud charges related to the Vitals hospitals scandal — will energise or embarrass the Labour ticket.

**Defining local issue:** The Marsaskala marina controversy has largely been resolved (plans shelved), but overdevelopment, coastal access, and the American University of Malta (AUM) Żonqor Point saga continue to resonate locally. Cost of living and housing are the dominant national themes.

*Key vacancy: Carmelo Mifsud Bonnici's votes (2022: ~1,000 first-count) are now up for grabs among three PN challengers.*
