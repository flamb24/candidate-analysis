### DISTRICT CONTEXT

District 13 is Gozo — Malta's sister island, which elects all 5 of its MPs through this single constituency. Historically, Gozo has been a PN stronghold, with PN regularly winning 3 of the 5 seats. However, the trend has been shifting: Labour has advanced from 43% in 2008 to ~48% in 2022, nearly tying the district. In 2022, Labour won 3 seats for the first time in modern history (Clint Camilleri, Anton Refalo, Jo Etienne Abela); PN won 2 (Alex Borg — then shadow Gozo minister — and Chris Said).

In 2026, the expected split has been revised: **with Alex Borg now PN leader and contesting Gozo as his primary district**, and Chris Said not contesting, the PN has the strongest possible D13 anchor. The question is whether PN can recapture its third seat. Internal polling cited in newsbook.com.mt (May 2026) suggested one of Labour's three Gozitan ministers could lose their seat.

**Labour:** Three sitting ministers — **Jo Etienne Abela** (Health Minister; D10 and D13), **Clint Camilleri** (Gozo and Planning Minister), **Anton Refalo** (Agriculture Minister) — plus two new candidates (Abigail Camilleri, George Paul Camilleri).

**PN:** Alex Borg (party leader), Norma Camilleri, Beppe Galea, Luke Said, Frank Anthony Tabone.

**Third parties:** Carmel Asciak (Momentum, D12 and D13); Paul Salomone (Aħwa Maltin leader, D2 and D13).

**Key Gozo issues:** Gozo Channel ferry service, overdevelopment on the island, the PN's promise to make Gozo a region (not just a ministry), healthcare access (new Gozo hospital pledge by PN), and cost of living as for the national electorate.

**Defining dynamic:** Alex Borg's presence at the top of the PN ticket — contesting both D12 and D13 — makes Gozo his most personal electoral battleground. Labour's three-minister ticket versus PN's leader-led slate is the defining D13 story.

**D13-specific notes:**
- Gozo's political identity is shaped by its island-specific concerns: ferry access, healthcare access (Gozo hospital vs Mater Dei), overdevelopment of the rural landscape, and the perpetual debate over whether Gozo needs a ministry, a region, or more autonomy.
- The Gozo Channel free-for-foot-passengers pledge by Labour (announced during Abela's Gozo campaign visit, May 2026) is a direct cost-of-living appeal to Gozitans.
- Alex Borg's Gozo origins (Nadur, raised in Fontana) give him genuine local credibility. His "make Gozo a region" pledge is the most specific structural Gozo policy offered by any party in 2026.
- Beppe Galea's endorsement by Metsola gives him elevated European profile for a D13 first-timer.
