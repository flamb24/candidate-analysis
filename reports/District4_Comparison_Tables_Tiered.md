### DISTRICT CONTEXT

District 4 covers the inner-south arc of Fgura, Gudja, Paola, Santa Luċija and Tarxien — a dense working-class and suburban Labour stronghold. Labour has consistently polled 65–70% here since 2013, with PN typically around 28–32%. The expected seat split is **4 Labour / 1 PN**, unchanged from 2022.

The defining story of D4 in 2026 is a crowded Labour ticket. With **Jonathan Attard** (Justice Minister, from Tarxien), **Chris Bonett** (Transport Minister), **Byron Camilleri** (Home Affairs Minister) and **Chris Fearne** all contesting — four ministers or former ministers on Labour's slate in a district that elects only four Labour MPs — the internal vote management challenge is acute. Internal party polling cited by The Shift News (May 2026) suggested Bonett was trailing rivals and at risk of losing his seat. Fearne, if elected here and on D3, would vacate one seat creating a casual election.

On the PN side, **Mark Anthony Sammut** is the incumbent and best hope. **Bernice Bonello** (D2 and D4), **Duncan Borg Myatt**, **Stefan Caruana** and **Michael Piccinino** (PN secretary general) round out the PN field. PN's structural ceiling is one seat.

**Defining local issues:** Traffic and road infrastructure (under Bonett's contested portfolio), population growth, cost of living, and housing. The PN's D4 campaign (per the Malta Independent, May 2026) focused heavily on population/immigration pressures.
