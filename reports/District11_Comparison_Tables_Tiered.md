### DISTRICT CONTEXT

District 11 covers Attard and Mosta — the only two-locality district in Malta, following boundary revisions approved by parliament in June 2025 (41-31, PL majority report) and gazetted in July 2025. Under those revisions, Mdina's 176 voters were transferred to D7 and Burmarrad was transferred to D12. This is a PN-leaning district: PN typically polls 52–55%, Labour 43–46%. Expected seat split: **3 PN / 2 Labour**. In 2022 PN took 3 seats (Bernard Grech, David Agius, Ivan Bartolo) and Labour 2 (Miriam Dalli, Alex Muscat).

**Key change from 2022:** David Agius, one of the three PN incumbents elected in 2022, is **not contesting in 2026**. His departure creates an open PN slot that the remaining candidates will compete to fill via transfers. Bernard Grech is also returning in a dramatically different capacity — **no longer party leader** (he resigned June 2025, succeeded by Alex Borg), contesting as a backbencher without the massive personal-vote premium a sitting leader commands. His vote is expected to drop significantly from 2022. This creates significant internal PN competition between Grech, Ivan Bartolo, Rebekah Borg, Dione Borg, Edmond Cuschieri and Joseph Mark Grech for three seats.

Labour's **Alex Muscat** and **Miriam Dalli** (D5 primary) are the Labour anchors. **Dalli's primary district is D5** in 2026, so her D11 presence is secondary; she may invest less heavily here than in 2022 when D11 was her primary.

**Key local issues:** Mosta tunnel and road infrastructure, overdevelopment in Attard and Mosta, traffic from residential sprawl.

**D11-specific notes:**
- David Agius's retirement opens a meaningful gap in the PN's D11 transfer pool. In 2022 he was elected as the third PN candidate; without him in the race, Grech's reduced vote and the resulting transfer cascade become the key variable determining whether Rebekah Borg or a second-tier PN candidate captures the third seat.
- Bernard Grech's vote is the most uncertain quantity in D11. In 2022, as party leader, he commanded enormous first-count votes across the island. In 2026 as a backbencher following a demoralising resignation, his personal vote will be substantially lower — the beneficiaries are likely to be Ivan Bartolo and Rebekah Borg.
- Miriam Dalli contesting D11 (her 2022 primary district) while also contesting D5 (as her 2026 primary) creates an interesting dynamic — she may not invest as heavily here this cycle, potentially putting the second Labour seat under mild pressure if her vote in D11 is suppressed relative to 2022.
- The revised boundaries (Mdina transferred to D7, Burmarrad to D12) mean D11 is now the most compact district in Malta — purely Attard and Mosta. The electoral impact of the removed localities is modest (Mdina had only ~176 voters), but D11 is now the only two-locality district in the country.
