### DISTRICT CONTEXT

District 11 covers Attard and Mosta — two of Malta's largest inland residential towns. This is a PN-leaning district: PN typically polls 52–55%, Labour 43–46%. Expected seat split: **3 PN / 2 Labour**. In 2022 PN took 3 seats (Bernard Grech, David Agius, Ivan Bartolo) and Labour 2 (Miriam Dalli, Alex Muscat).

In 2026, the defining change is **Bernard Grech no longer being party leader** — he resigned in June 2025 after dismal polls and was succeeded by Alex Borg. Grech is still contesting D11 as a backbencher, but without the massive personal-vote premium a sitting leader commands. His vote is expected to drop significantly from 2022. This creates internal PN competition between Grech, Ivan Bartolo, Rebekah Borg, Dione Borg, and Edmond Cuschieri and Joseph Mark Grech for three seats.

Labour's **Alex Muscat** and **Miriam Dalli** (D5 primary) are the Labour anchors. **Dalli's primary district is D5**, so her D11 presence is as a secondary. The district heavily features the PN candidate bench.

**Key local issues:** Mosta tunnel and road infrastructure, overdevelopment in Attard and Mosta, traffic from residential sprawl.

**D11-specific notes:**
- Bernard Grech's vote is the most uncertain quantity in D11. In 2022, as party leader, he commanded enormous first-count votes across the island. In 2026 as a regular backbencher following a demoralising resignation, his personal vote will be redistributed — potentially benefiting Ivan Bartolo and the other PN candidates who absorb his transfers.
- The PN's challenge is to elect all three seats despite Grech's diminished pull. Ivan Bartolo and Rebekah Borg are the most likely beneficiaries.
- Miriam Dalli contesting D11 (her 2022 primary district) while also contesting D5 (as her 2026 primary) creates an interesting dynamic — she may not invest as heavily in D11 this cycle.
