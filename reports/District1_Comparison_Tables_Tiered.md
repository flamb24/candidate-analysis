### DISTRICT CONTEXT

District 1 is Labour's southernmost urban stronghold — covering the historic capital Valletta, its port-side satellites Ħamrun and Marsa, and the inner-harbour belt including Floriana, Pietà and Santa Venera. Labour has dominated this district since 2013, winning 55–58% of the vote in 2013–2022 while the PN has typically polled 40–43%. The expected seat split is **3 Labour / 2 PN**, consistent with results since 2013. A Labour 4th seat would require a national swing exceeding any plausible scenario; a PN 3rd seat is structurally out of reach absent a historic collapse.

Overdevelopment, gentrification of Valletta, and over-tourism are the defining local issues, alongside the national concerns of cost of living, population growth, and corruption. Valletta is under a UNESCO state-of-conservation reporting requirement due December 2026. The newly redrawn district boundary now includes part of Birkirkara (Fleur-de-Lys), which slightly broadens the pool.

*Note: Mario de Marco (PN), long-serving D1 MP since 2003, confirmed he will not contest 2026, creating significant open space on the PN's seat. This is the most consequential vacancy in the district.*
