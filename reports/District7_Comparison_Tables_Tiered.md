### DISTRICT CONTEXT

District 7 is the central-west rural belt of Malta — Rabat, Żebbuġ, Dingli, Mdina and environs. Labour has consistently won 55–60% since 2013 (up from ~50% pre-2013). Expected split: **3 Labour / 2 PN**. In 2022 Labour took 3 seats and PN 2 (Adrian Delia and Ryan Callus — both retiring/not contesting D7 primarily in 2026).

The defining feature of D7 in 2026 is the **PN's significant candidate vacancy**: Ryan Callus is not contesting and Adrian Delia has moved primarily to D8. **Rebekah Borg** is the new PN D7 anchor. Labour's **Ian Borg** (Deputy PM, D6 and D7) is the most prominent candidate. **Naomi Cachia** (Government Whip) and **Julia Farrugia** (Inclusion Minister, D5 and D7) add ministerial weight to Labour's slate.

**Key local issues:** Overdevelopment pressures (particularly Żebbuġ and Rabat), hunting and trapping community (significant in D7), and rural heritage/planning.
